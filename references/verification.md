# Evidence and financial verification

## Sources and freshness
- Confirm issuer/entity and exchange; ticker alone may be ambiguous.
- Record publisher, actual URL, document title/location, publication date if known, retrieval timestamp, reporting period and units.
- Open original contents supporting material figures. Never promote a search snippet, inaccessible table or OCR guess into a confirmed fact.
- Prefer original sources for facts. Assess their limitations; management's presentation is not independent validation.
- A straightforward official fact may rely on one original source. Material disputed claims require corroboration or explicit uncertainty.
- Syndicated copies and sources quoting the same original are not independent evidence.
- Distinguish event date, publication date, reporting period and retrieval date. Recheck revisions. State delayed/unknown timing for prices.
- If evidence is conflicting, examine scope, date and methodology before selecting a value. Do not average incompatible figures.

## Calculation and comparability
Use an available deterministic tool for material calculations. Preserve inputs, formula and assumptions. If none is available, show simple arithmetic, label the tool limitation and avoid complex unsupported numerical conclusions.

- Match currency, scaling, reporting period, fiscal calendar, consolidated/parent scope, attribution and continuing/discontinued operations.
- YoY/QoQ for positive bases: (current / comparable - 1) * 100. Never compare a quarter against YTD as if both are quarterly.
- Zero base: percentage growth is undefined; show the absolute change.
- Negative base: explain loss widening/narrowing or profit turnaround using levels and absolute change; avoid misleading conventional growth percentages.
- Rates: distinguish percentage points from percent change. A rate from 5% to 6% rises 1 percentage point, or 20% relatively.
- Derived Q4 = compatible annual minus nine-month result. Align revision/accounting basis first; label the quarter as derived and check annual-only adjustments.
- Separate revenue, operating income, net income and attributable earnings. Do not substitute EBITDA for cash flow or profit.
- Show the reconciliation behind adjusted profit. Do not assume excluded costs are nonrecurring.
- Define free cash flow and net debt. Document capex and lease-treatment choices where relevant.
- For currencies, disclose conversion rate/date and whether a comparison mixes exchange rates.
- Prefer latest comparable official revisions; explain important historical changes.

## Specialized checks
Trade: distinguish arrivals from departures and exports from imports; preserve origin/destination, HS product, wet/dry tonnes, cumulative versus monthly, report windows and coverage. Do not sum overlapping weekly windows. A forecast from partial arrivals is an estimate with methodology, not an official monthly result.

Valuation: verify price timestamp, currency, shares, dilution, debt/cash, metric period and methodology. Do not mix market cap with enterprise-value multiples. If inputs are missing, no unsupported valuation precision.

Consensus: identify provider, date, sample/coverage when available and metric basis. One analyst is not automatically consensus. Mark unavailable consensus; do not replace it with your own forecast without labeling it.

## Final claim audit
For each material claim:
1. Does the cited evidence directly support it?
2. Are date, entity, scope and units correct?
3. Does the calculation reconcile to the inputs?
4. Is an inference or forecast labeled and supported by a mechanism?
5. Is conflicting/missing evidence visible where it changes the conclusion?
6. Is the strongest relevant counterargument represented?
7. Are report length, coverage, channel format and delivery aligned to this assignment?

Facts can be confirmed while implications remain uncertain. Do not assign precise confidence percentages without a defensible method.

## Synthetic walkthrough
Fixture revised-series supplies preliminary prior 100, revised prior 120, and current 150. Use 120 as the comparable baseline; compute (150 / 120 - 1) * 100 = 25%. Mention the revision and cite the fixture IDs. Do not use 50% from the obsolete preliminary baseline. Record formula and inputs if persistence is available. This illustrates the procedure; it is not proof of actual-agent performance.
